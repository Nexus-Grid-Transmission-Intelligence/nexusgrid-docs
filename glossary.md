# Glossary

This glossary defines core Nexus Grid terms in plain English.

## Topology

Topology is the way infrastructure elements are connected to each other. In Nexus Grid materials, topology helps explain why one restoration path may depend on another path or condition.

## Dependency

A dependency is something that must be true, available, or restored before another modeled step can proceed. Dependencies can change the order of recovery.

## Replay

Replay is the ability to review a modeled event after it runs. Replay helps show what happened, in what order, and where the sequence changed or slowed.

## Sequencing

Sequencing is the order of modeled restoration or coordination steps. Sequencing visibility helps reviewers understand why one step comes before another.

## Propagation

Propagation is how a modeled effect moves through connected infrastructure relationships. It can show where recovery progresses, localizes, or stalls.

## Restoration Plateau

A restoration plateau is a point where modeled recovery progress slows or stops because constraints, dependencies, or topology limits prevent easy continuation.

## Operational Trace

An operational trace is structured evidence generated during a modeled run. It records sequence behavior, replay events, progression, and other review details.

## Coordination Boundary

A coordination boundary is a point where recovery behavior depends on a constraint, dependency, or decision context that may require review. It marks where coordination becomes more important or more difficult.
